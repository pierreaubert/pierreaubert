## Selected projects / 01—04

### 01 / Audio engineering — SOUND OF THE FUTURE

### SotF

`RUST · AUDIO DSP`

Better sound, from playback to room correction. An audio player, measurement tools, automatic EQ, and a flexible collection of audio plugins.

[Discover SotF ↗](https://sotf.spinorama.org) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/sotf) · [Contribute ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/sotf/issues)

---

### 02 / Native interfaces — COMPONENTS → APPLICATIONS

### gpui-toolkit

`RUST · GPUI`

A toolkit for expressive native applications. Reusable UI components, design tokens, charting primitives, and tooling built around GPUI.

[Try the live demos ↗](https://gpui-toolkit.spinorama.org/) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/gpui-toolkit) · [Contribute ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/gpui-toolkit/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)

---

### 03 / Computational physics — GEOMETRY → PHYSICS → OPTIMISATION

### Sonium

`RUST · FEM / BEM`

Numerical tools for the physical world. Multi-physics FEM and BEM solvers, mesh generation, and shape and topology optimisation in an open source Rust core.

[Explore Sonium ↗](https://sonium.spinorama.org) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/sonium) · [Contribute ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/sonium/issues)

---

### 04 / Acoustic measurements — LESS GUESSWORK. MORE MEASUREMENT.

### Spinorama

`PYTHON · DATA VISUALISATION`

Understand loudspeakers through measurement. Browse, compare, and analyse speaker data, with frequency response plots and documentation to guide your exploration.

[Explore the database ↗](https://www.spinorama.org/) · [Source ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/spinorama) · [Contribute ↗](https://github.com/pierreaubert/spinorama/issues)

---

## The common thread

### Understand it. Build it. Share it.

From loudspeaker measurements to physical simulation, these projects explore the space between mathematical models and practical tools. Native interfaces make the results accessible. Open source makes the work available to inspect, question, and extend.
